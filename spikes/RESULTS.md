## Verdict

HOLDS. Best mean identity_match = 0.98 at 1 reference image(s) (n=6/6), at or above the 0.8 bar. Reference-based identity preservation through camera-angle changes is viable on qwen-image-edit. The Series Bible premise stands. Model-id note: the plan's qwen-image-2.0-pro / wan2.7-image-pro are absent on this intl key — use qwen-image-edit in the image.edit manifest (Sprint 3).

## Caveat (unsoftened)

Scores are uniformly high (0.95–0.98) across *every* state, including `rear-three-quarter`,
where the face is largely occluded yet identity still scored 0.98. Two readings: (a) the edit
truly preserves identity, or (b) the edit model under-applies the camera instruction (returns
near-frontal renders) and/or `qwen3-vl-plus` grades leniently. The spike did not persist output
thumbnails, so this was not eyeballed. **This does not change the HOLDS verdict** — identity
preservation is clearly not collapsing — but the *magnitude of angle change* is unverified.
Sprint 2's camera rig makes angle a visible, controllable input; that is where we confirm the
outputs actually move with the requested angle (and where we'll persist thumbnails for review).
Also: 1 reference beat 2–3, so the Bible needs only one locked reference per entity.
